# Yoko Yoko / Counterirritant Liniment — Safe Use

A common Malaysian counterirritant for muscle soreness (DOMS). Works by
heat/counter-irritation; used correctly it aids recovery, used wrongly it
can chemically burn the skin.

## Best usage
- Apply **30–60 minutes before sleep, after showering**, for DOMS/muscle
  soreness in calves, quads, hamstrings, glutes.
- Wash hands after applying; keep away from eyes and open skin.
- Small amount, rub in fully.

## Contraindications (never apply)
- Right before sweating or heat (running, gym, sauna) — heat + liniment =
  skin irritation/burn.
- On fresh sharp joint pain or acute tendonitis (the tendon is already
  inflamed; the liniment adds heat).
- Under tight compression garments (traps heat against skin).
- Immediately after a hot shower (pores open, skin sensitized).
- Broken skin, rashes, or recent scar tissue.

## DOMS vs injury — the distinction that matters
- **DOMS:** symmetrical, dull aching muscle tightness; fades after warming
  up; both legs equally. Liniment appropriate.
- **Injury warning:** unilateral, pinpoint sharp pain or bone tenderness;
  worsens during activity or when walking down stairs. NO liniment — see
  the physio triage rules (triage.md).
