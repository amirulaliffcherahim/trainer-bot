# Injury Triage & Red Flags

## DOMS vs injury (first filter, before anything else)
- **DOMS:** symmetrical, dull aching muscle tightness; fades after warming
  up. Manage with rest-day rules, stretching, liniment (see yoko_yoko.md).
- **Injury:** unilateral, pinpoint sharp pain or bone tenderness; worsens
  during activity or walking down stairs. Treat as injury — load
  management, not liniment.

## Absolute red flags (see a professional NOW, stop training)
- Chest pain, pressure, or tightness during/after exercise
- Difficulty breathing, shortness of breath at rest
- Fainting, collapse, or blackouts
- Fever or illness with training within the same week
- Numbness, tingling, or weakness in a limb
- Inability to weight-bear after an incident
- Joint swelling that appears suddenly after an incident
- Hearing/feeling a "pop" followed by pain

Any of these: stop. Do not run. Do not "test it out". See a doctor.

## The triage question the bot asks
1. Where is the pain? (quad, shin, knee, calf, ankle, hip, other)
2. Sharp or dull? Sharp = stop; dull/ache = manage.
3. Did it come on suddenly (incident) or gradually (overuse)?
4. Is there swelling, bruising, or weakness?
5. Does it hurt at rest or only when running?

## Load management while injured
- Pain 0–2/10 during the activity and settles after: keep going at reduced
  volume (50–70%).
- Pain 3–5/10 and persistent: stop the aggravating session, substitute easy
  work, and treat (see rehab files).
- Pain 6+/10, sharp, or worsening during a session: stop immediately, rest,
  professional review.
- Never "run through" pain that gets worse as the run progresses.

## General rule for the panel
- When in doubt, the physio's default is REST. Recovery weeks cost less than
  injury months. The physio's veto overrides every other persona.
