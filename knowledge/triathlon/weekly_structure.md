# Triathlon Weekly Training Structure

> Source: T100 Triathlon — "How to Train for Triathlons: Everything You Need
> to Know" (t100triathlon.com/articles/training/how-to-train-for-triathlons/)

Consistency beats 'hero' sessions that leave you too drained to train
tomorrow. Balance volume, intensity and recovery across swim, bike, run,
strength and rest.

## First-timer week (T100 / 100 km, ~6–7 h·wk⁻¹ for 12 weeks)
- **Swim:** 1 × 30 min short intervals (e.g. 8 × 100 m or 4 × 200 m); 1 ×
  45 min endurance + technique.
- **Bike:** 2 × 30 min intervals (e.g. 4 × 5 min hard, 2 min easy between) or
  1 × 60 min; plus 1 × 2 h endurance ride.
- **Run:** 2 × 30 min (1 interval-based, 1 tempo); plus 1 × 1 h endurance run.
- **Strength & core:** 5–10 min core, balance or mobility before 3–4 sessions.
- **Rest:** 1 full rest day per week; take 1–2 extra rest days every few weeks
  when your body asks.
- **Recovery aids:** regular stretching and foam rolling, especially legs.

## Experienced week (10+ h·wk⁻¹)
- **Swim:** 2–3 × 45–60 min. Alternate endurance (500–1000 m repeats) with
  technique/intervals (100–400 m reps at threshold to tempo intensity).
- **Bike:** 3 × 60 min mixing tempo, sweet spot and threshold intervals —
  30–40 min of total effort per session with recovery between; plus 1 × 3 h
  endurance ride (chat in short sentences).
- **Run:** 2–3 × 60 min — longer tempo runs (e.g. 4 × 10 min) and shorter
  threshold intervals (e.g. 10 × 4 min); plus 1 × 90 min–2 h easy
  conversational endurance run.
- **Strength & core:** 1–2 × 45 min — core stability, bodyweight strength,
  balance, mobility. Swap one 60 min run for strength every other week.
- **Rest:** 1 full rest day; extra rest days as needed.

## Scaling any plan down or up
- Too hard to start: walk/run approach, shorter intervals, more endurance work
  first — build a solid foundation.
- Comfortable already: extend sessions, add fuller strength sessions, or
  lengthen intervals as you progress.
- Avoid sudden jumps in intensity or duration; keep an eye on consistency.

## 70.3 weekly balance
- **Swim:** 2–3 sessions — endurance and open-water skills.
- **Bike:** 3 rides — one long endurance, one interval, one brick with a run.
- **Run:** 3 sessions — long runs blended with tempo and transition runs.
- **Strength & core:** 2 sessions for resilience and stability.
- **Rest/active recovery:** 1–2 days.
- Missing the occasional workout won't derail a 70.3; quitting consistency
  will.

## Sprint triathlon (750 m / 20 km / 5 km)
- Shorter distance → intensity matters more than endless mileage; speed
  development is as important as endurance.
- Key workouts: short sharp intervals in pool/bike/run; bike-to-run brick
  sessions for 'jelly legs'; race simulation — practising transitions in real
  conditions cuts race-day stress.

## Scheduling around a 9-to-5 life
- Work availability shapes structure: shorter sessions before/after work, or
  load certain days and rest in between.
- Indoor options (treadmill, bike trainer) give a good time-to-benefit ratio —
  workload stays consistent with less freewheeling, so you get more done in
  less time.
