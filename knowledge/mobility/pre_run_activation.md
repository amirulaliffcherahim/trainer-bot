# Pre-Run Activation (5–8 min, every run)

## Why
- Tropical-heat mornings + sitting all day = sleepy hips and stiff ankles.
  Activation wakes the running muscles and protects the knees/shins.

## The routine (before every run)
1. Leg swings — 10/side front-back, 10/side side-to-side (30 s)
2. Glute bridges — 10, hold 2 s at top (20 s)
3. Walking lunges — 8/side, upright torso (30 s)
4. Ankle circles — 10 each way per ankle (30 s)
5. Calf raises — 15 slow, full range (30 s)
6. 2–3 min of easy walking/jogging into the run

## When tightness is present
- Add 10 more glute bridges and 20 s of banded side-steps (monster walks)
  before the run if the athlete reports quad or hip tightness.
- If tightness turns into sharp pain during the warm-up: stop and check with
  the physio persona. Activation is not pain relief.

## Notes
- Static stretching before running: NO. Long holds before a run reduce power.
  Static work belongs after the run or on rest days.
- The whole routine is 5–8 minutes — non-negotiable on long-run Saturdays.
