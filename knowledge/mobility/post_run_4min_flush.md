# Quick 4-Minute Post-Run Flush

Ultra-fast cooldown routine for immediately after a run before showering:

- **Standing Quad Pull**: 2 × 30s per leg (keeps quads supple, reduces patellar strain)
- **Wall Calf Stretch**: 2 × 30s per leg (straight knee for gastrocnemius, bent knee for soleus)
- **Deep Squat Pry / Hold**: 45s breathing steady (opens hips and decompresses lower back)
- **Ankle Rolls**: 10 slow circles each direction (restores ankle mobility after pounding pavement)

## Why it works
Takes under 4 minutes, prevents blood pooling in the legs, and cuts down post-run tightness without needing yoga mats or equipment.
