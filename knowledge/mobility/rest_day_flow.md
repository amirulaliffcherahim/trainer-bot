# Rest Day 5-Minute Recovery Flow

Light restorative mobility routine for scheduled rest days:

- **Cat-Cow Breathing**: 8 slow cycles (mobilizes the spine and relieves tension)
- **90/90 Hip Switches**: 8 smooth reps per side (restores internal and external hip rotation)
- **Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch**: 30s per side (counters prolonged sitting and tight hip flexors)
- **Easy Hamstring Sweeps**: 10 dynamic sweeps per side (gentle nerve flossing and hamstring mobility)

## Rest Day Law
Rest days are when adaptation and muscle repair happen. Keep the flow gentle and relaxing; do not turn mobility into a workout.
