# Cool-Down & Tightness Management

## Post-run (5–10 min)
1. 5 min easy walk/jog to bring the heart rate down gradually
2. Static holds (30–45 s × 2–3 per side) — ONLY after the run or on rest days:
   - Quad stretch (standing or side-lying) — the athlete's #1 tight area
   - Calf stretch (straight + bent knee) — protects the Achilles
   - Hip flexor stretch (half-kneeling)
   - Glute/piriformis stretch (figure-4)
3. 5 min foam rolling (optional): quads, IT band, calves — 30 s per area,
   never roll through bone or joints

## The 2×45s quad rule
- Standard standing recommendation: **2×45 s quad stretch per side after every
  session**, plus on rest days if tight.
- Quad tightness is a known precursor to the athlete's tendonitis pattern —
  consistent stretching is the cheap insurance.

## When tightness is more than tightness
- Constant ache that worsens overnight, swelling, or pain when walking
  normally → stop stretching the area and check with the physio persona.
- Stretching should feel like mild pull, never sharp pain. Sharp = stop.
