# Tight Calf & Betis Release Routine

Quick 4-minute targeted routine for calf (betis) and Achilles tightness after runs in the heat:

- **Downward Dog Calf Pedal**: 10 slow alternating pedals per side (dynamic stretch through gastrocnemius)
- **Wall Soleus Stretch**: 2 × 30s per side with bent back knee (targets the deep soleus muscle)
- **Seated Towel/Strap Calf Pull**: 30s per leg with toes pulled toward the shin
- **Ankle Alphabet / Rotations**: 10 reps each foot (enhances blood flow and clears stiffness)

## Notes for Betis / Calf Care
- Hot, humid runs cause extra cramping and tightness due to sweat/electrolyte loss — rehydrate with sodium/potassium.
- If calf tightness turns into sharp localized pain or Achilles tenderness, stop stretching and shift to isometrics.
