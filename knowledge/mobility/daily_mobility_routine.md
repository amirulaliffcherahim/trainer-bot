# Daily 10-Minute Head-to-Toe Mobility Routine

Do this daily — morning or evening, on rest days and training days alike.
10 minutes, no equipment.

1. **Neck circles:** 5× each direction
2. **Standing T-spine rotations:** 8× each side
3. **Cat-cow stretch:** 8 slow cycles
4. **World's Greatest Stretch:** 5× each side
5. **Kneeling hip flexor stretch:** 30 s each side
6. **90/90 hip switches:** 8 smooth switches side-to-side
7. **Deep squat hold & ankle shift:** 60 s
8. **Ankle circles & toe spreads:** 10× each foot
9. **Calf & shin stretch:** 30 s each leg

## Notes
- The athlete sits a lot (KL office life) — hips and ankles are the
  tightest links; steps 5–7 are the non-negotiables.
- This routine is separate from the pre-run activation (5–8 min dynamic set)
  and the post-run cool-down (static holds). All three have different jobs.
- Shin stretch (step 9) doubles as tibialis loading — shin splint prevention.
