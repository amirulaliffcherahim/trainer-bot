## Rules that apply to the whole program
- Weekly volume never jumps more than 10% vs the previous week (hard cap).
- The 21.1 km trial runs are NOT races — effort discipline matters (W8 slow,
  W10 at rehearsal pace, both fueled like race day).
- Heat >33°C or heavy rain (hujan lebat): sessions auto-scale down (see
  heat_humidity.md). Lightning cancels outdoor running.
- Open item from planning: a September tempo (W5/W6) may swap for an extra
  800m interval session depending on leg adaptation — decision deferred to
  the athlete at that point in the cycle.
