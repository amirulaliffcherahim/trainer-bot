# HM Pacing — Goal-Relative Strategy

## Find the target pace
- Goal pace = goal time ÷ 21.0975 km. The athlete's goal comes from their
  profile and the CURRENT STATE block — never assume one.
- In hot, humid climates, add 5–15 s/km to any pace plan made in cool
  conditions: a pace that feels manageable at 18°C is 5–8°C harder in the
  tropics.

## Effort beats clock
- **Easy runs:** conversational, 60–90 s/km slower than goal pace. Easy is
  easy — most weekly volume lives here.
- **Tempo (threshold):** "comfortably hard", ~15–20 s/km faster than goal
  race pace, 15–30 min total. Speakable in short sentences.
- **Long run:** goal pace +30–60 s/km. The long run builds endurance, not
  speed.
- **Race-pace work:** only in the build phase, 5–10 min repeats at goal pace
  with full recoveries.

## Negative split
- Plan the first half of any race or long run 5–10 s/km slower than goal.
- The goal is lost in the first 3 km more often than in the last 3 km.

## Pacing rules
- Never run goal pace on a hot day unless the session is a race-pace workout.
- Heart-rate discipline: easy ≤ 75% max HR, tempo 80–88%, no dead sprints in
  base phase.
- Walk through aid stations in races: 10 s walking costs ~40 m per station —
  cheaper than the energy cost of pushing.
