# HM Pacing — SELMAR 2:30:00 Strategy

## The targets (authoritative)
- SELMAR primary: **2:30:00** = 2:29:47 @ **7:06 min/km** average.
- Safe backup: **2:45:00** = 7:49 min/km.
- TwinCity (Jan 24 2027): refinement toward sub-2:30 / 2:25 (6:50–7:00/km).
- In KL heat and humidity, add 5–15 s/km to any pace plan made in cool
  conditions. A 7:06 target in 28°C feels like 6:50 in 18°C.

## SELMAR race-day pacing (target 2:30:00)

| Segment | Pace | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| KM 1–3: controlled warm-up | ~7:20/km | ~00:22:00 |
| KM 4–10: target rhythm | ~7:05/km | ~01:11:35 |
| KM 11–16: cruising efficiency | ~7:05/km | ~01:54:05 |
| KM 17–21.1: finish push | ~7:00/km | **02:29:47** |

The first 3 km are deliberately slower — the sub-2:30 goal is lost in the
first 3 km more often than in the last 3 km.

## Training paces (all based on the above)
- **Easy runs:** 7:45–8:15/km (conversational). Most weekly volume lives here.
- **Tempo (threshold):** 6:40–6:45/km, "comfortably hard", 15–30 min total.
- **Intervals:** 4×800m @ ~6:30/km with 2 min jog rest (program Week 7);
  later 800s @ 6:30/km in the TwinCity build.
- **Long run:** 8:00/km baseline, peak segments at 7:05–7:10/km in build weeks.
- **Trial runs:** W8 = 21.1 km @ 8:00/km low-stress; W10 = 21.1 km @ 7:10/km
  race-pace rehearsal.

## Pacing rules
- Never run target pace on a hot day unless the session is a race-pace
  workout.
- Heart-rate discipline: easy ≤ 75% max HR, tempo 80–88%.
- Walk through aid stations in races: 10 s walking costs ~40 m per station —
  cheaper than the energy cost of pushing.
- Negative split every race and long run: first half 5–10 s/km slower.
