# Rest Day Recovery Rules (Wed & Sun — non-negotiable)

## The rules
- Rest days are FULL rest from running. **No recovery runs on rest days** —
  running impact is 2.5–3× body weight; a "recovery run" still loads bones,
  tendons, and joints that Wed/Sun exist to repair.
- Active mobility instead: 20–30 min walks + the daily 10-min head-to-toe
  routine + light stretching.
- Protein: 20–30 g every 3–4 hours, totaling ~99–110 g/day (surplus diet
  ~2,700 kcal/day — the athlete is underweight and must eat to recover).
- Sleep: 7.5–9 hours. Sleep is the #1 recovery tool; a missed session is
  cheaper than a missed night.

## Why
- Tendons and bones adapt 3–6 months behind cardiovascular fitness
  (tendon/bone lag). The rest days are where that adaptation happens.
- The 2-day rest gap (Wed + Sun) brackets the week's hardest work: Sat long
  run → Sun rest → Mon light upper → Tue quality.
