# Bodyweight Strength Progressions

## The athlete's fixed exercise list
- **Upper (Mon):** Military Push Up, Chair Dip, Australian Pull Up, Diamond
  Push Up, Pike Hold
- **Lower (Thu):** Squat, Bulgarian Split Squat, Calf Raise, **Tibial Raise**
  (shin splint prevention — do these every leg day), Side-to-Side Squat
- **Core (Fri):** Plank, Side Plank, Mountain Climber, Leg Raises, Glute
  Bridges
- **Form drills (Fri, optional 5 min):** High Knees, Butt Kicks, A-Skips,
  2×50m Strides

## Push (Mon upper)
- Level 1: incline push-ups (hands on bench/wall) 3×10
- Level 2: standard push-ups 3×8–12, slow negatives (3 s down)
- Level 3: deficit/feet-elevated push-ups 3×8–10
- Advance when current level is clean for the top rep range.

## Pull (Mon upper)
- Level 1: horizontal rows (table/bar at hip height) 3×10
- Level 2: negative pull-ups (3–5 s lowering) 3×5
- Level 3: full pull-ups 3×5–8, then weighted/one-arm progressions
- Pull strength protects the shoulders and balances the push volume.

## Squat (Thu legs)
- Level 1: bodyweight squats, full depth, 3×12
- Level 2: tempo squats (3 s down, 1 s pause) 3×10, pistol progression
  (assisted) once balance allows
- Level 3: single-leg work — split squats / assisted pistols 3×8/side
- Leg strength is the runner's injury armor (knees, shins, hips).

## Core (Fri core)
- Level 1: plank 3×30–45 s, dead bug 3×10/side
- Level 2: plank 3×60 s, hollow-body holds 3×20–30 s
- Level 3: plank with leg lifts, side plank 3×30 s/side, hanging knee raises
- A braced core keeps the pelvis stable for 21 km — priority, not garnish.

## Overload rules
- One variable at a time: reps → sets → tempo → leverage.
- Form breaks → drop a level; never chase numbers through sloppy reps.
- Joint pain during a movement = stop that movement, ask the physio persona.
- Sessions 25–40 min. Mon upper + Fri core stay light before Sat long run.
